# Hamnljus · Harbor Light

A cozy, story-driven 2D platformer RPG set in the sleepy Swedish harbor village of Saltvik. Everything is in a single file: open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** You inherit Grandma Ingrid's farm. Restore the harbor bit by bit and relight the old lighthouse (21-step main quest plus 26 side quests).
- **Activities:** farming (20 crops, 4 seasons, greenhouse), fishing (38 species incl. legendaries, reel minigame), foraging, mining, tree chopping, cooking (28 dishes), crafting, artisan machines, animals.
- **World:** 8 areas (farm, harbor, forest, beach, cliffs, cave, Lighthouse Isle, greenhouse), day/night cycle, weather, 4 seasonal festivals.
- **People:** 12 villagers with schedules, friendship hearts, gift tastes and rewards.
- **Long-term goals:** 9 harbor projects, museum, 30 hidden golden shells, daily requests, 22 achievements, tool upgrades.
- **Languages:** Svenska and English (switch on the title screen or in Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard, touch (on-screen D-pad and buttons) and gamepad. Gamepad: A jump / back, B use & talk / confirm, Y menu, LB/RB cycle items. Keyboard: E confirm, Space back. Touch: ✋ confirm, ⤒ back. Fullscreen is on by default (entered on your first tap/click; toggle in Settings).
- **Saving:** autosaves to browser storage every 30 seconds, when you sleep, and when the tab is hidden; "Save & exit" in the menu returns to the title screen. Saves can be exported/imported as text in Settings.

# Aetherfall · Rise of the Hollow Tide

An original open-world fantasy MMORPG-style game in a single file: open `aetherfall.html` in any modern desktop browser (works offline; Three.js is embedded).

- **World:** one seamless continent with three zones (The Thornwood, Saltmere Coast, The Ember Wastes), three cities, roads, waystones for fast travel, a day/night cycle and a Skyhorn mount.
- **Characters:** character creator with 5 ancestries, 2 body types, height, skin, 6 hairstyles, hair and eye colors.
- **Jobs:** 7 switchable jobs, each levelled separately to 30: Warden and Berserker (tanks), Mender and Starseer (healers), Duelist, Ranger and Spellweaver (DPS). Combat uses a 2.5s global cooldown, combos, cast times, oGCD abilities, enmity and ground telegraphs.
- **Content:** a 15-quest main story, 9 side quests, rifts (dynamic world events with gold/silver/bronze rewards), 3 four-player dungeons and an eight-player trial, all filled with AI party members through the Expedition Board. Bosses use cones, donuts, line attacks, stack and spread markers, tankbusters, adds and enrage timers.
- **Living world:** simulated adventurers that wander, fight, emote and chat.
- **Economy:** gear tiers with item levels, shops, gathering nodes, and a crafting minigame with high-quality (HQ) results.
- **Controls:** WASD to move, drag the mouse to look, click or Tab to target, 1–0, - and = for actions (add Shift for the second bar), F to interact, R to mount, and C/I/K/J/M/B for menus. Press H in-game for the full list.
- **Saving:** autosaves to browser storage; you can export or import a save from the system menu (Esc).
