# Hamnljus · Harbor Light

A cozy, story-driven 2D platformer RPG set in the sleepy Swedish harbor village of Saltvik. Everything is in a single file: open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** You inherit Grandma Ingrid's farm. Restore the harbor bit by bit and relight the old lighthouse (21-step main quest plus 26 side quests).
- **Activities:** farming (20 crops, 4 seasons, greenhouse), fishing (38 species incl. legendaries, reel minigame), foraging, mining, tree chopping, cooking (28 dishes), crafting, artisan machines, animals.
- **World:** 8 areas (farm, harbor, forest, beach, cliffs, cave, Lighthouse Isle, greenhouse), day/night cycle, weather, 4 seasonal festivals.
- **People:** 12 villagers with schedules, friendship hearts, gift tastes and rewards.
- **Long-term goals:** 9 harbor projects, museum, 30 hidden golden shells, daily requests, 22 achievements, tool upgrades.
- **Languages:** Svenska and English (switch on the title screen or in Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard, touch (on-screen D-pad and buttons) and gamepad. Gamepad: A jump / back, B use & talk / confirm, Y menu, LB/RB cycle items. Keyboard: E confirm, Space back. Touch: ✋ confirm, ⤒ back. Fullscreen is on by default (entered on your first tap/click; toggle in Settings).
- **Saving:** autosaves to browser storage every 30 seconds, when you sleep, and when the tab is hidden; "Save & exit" in the menu returns to the title screen. Saves can be exported/imported as text in Settings.

# Emberhollow · Glödhollow

A dark, cozy, story-driven top-down survival crafting RPG in a single file: open `emberhollow.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** The sun has faded into the Long Dusk. Relight Hollowmere's Great Lantern by recovering five Ember Shards from guardians in the frozen north, the whispering east, the drowned south, the ashen west and the bottom of the Deepdark Mine (15-step main quest, 35 side quests, daily notice-board requests, 10 lore pages, ending and post-game).
- **Survival:** health, hunger, warmth (cold nights, winter, snowfields, campfires, warm clothing) and energy; dynamic lighting with torches, lanterns and lamps; day/night, 4 seasons, weather.
- **Content:** 230+ items, 127 recipes at 8 stations, 24 research nodes (study items for research points), 14 crops, 12 fish with a timing minigame, tool tiers from wood to emberite, weapons, staffs, armor, trinkets, building (walls, floors, furniture, chests, sprinklers, beehives, coops, kegs), endless procedural mine with lifts, 11 enemy types and 5 bosses, 11 villagers with shops, gifts and friendship rewards, skills, feats and a collection log.
- **Languages:** English and Svenska (title screen or Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard + mouse, touch (virtual stick and buttons), and gamepad for both play and all menus. Fullscreen is requested on start.
- **Saving:** autosaves every 30 seconds, on sleep and when the tab is hidden.
- **Splash:** opens with the same 10-second Cozy Forest splash as Starhaven.
- **Art:** round, smooth vector-style graphics (soft rounded terrain, gradient trees and rocks, chibi characters, glossy creatures) with dark, glowing lighting.
