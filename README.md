# Frostlight Station

A cozy, first-person, low-poly 3D open-world RPG set on an arctic research station. Everything is in one file: open `frostlight.html` in any modern browser (desktop or mobile). It uses no external libraries; it has its own WebGL renderer, and every icon, portrait and logo is drawn in code (no emoji).

- **Story:** You join a small crew at 78 degrees north. Restore power, fix the radio, set up weather probes, drill ice cores, photograph the aurora, uncover the lost 1911 Vega Borealis expedition through 12 journal pages, bridge the crevasse and reach the Ice Cave and the Aurora Heart (15-step main quest).
- **Side content:** 40+ side quests, daily requests, 10 station projects (sauna, pier extension, dog sled travel, greenhouse wing, auto-mist, wind turbine, hot spring, lighthouse, telescope, glacier bridge), 14 research upgrades, a museum, and 28 achievements.
- **Activities:** fishing (33 species including 5 legendaries, with ice fishing through drilled holes and a reeling minigame), greenhouse and outdoor farming (17 crops), foraging, chopping, mining (Old Mine, glacier, ice cave), smelting, crafting (60+ recipes, including tool upgrades and warm clothing), cooking (30+ dishes), wildlife photography (16 species), placeable campfires, chests, lamps, heaters, planters and more.
- **Survival, cozy style:** warmth drops outdoors (faster at night and in storms). Warm up indoors or by fires, wear insulated clothing and eat hot food.
- **World:** a large island with the station, tundra lake, spruce grove, frozen bay, mountains, glacier and crevasse, a day/night cycle, four seasons (Thaw, Midnight Sun, Frostfall, Polar Night) with changing terrain, weather (snow, blizzards) and northern lights, plus a festival every season.
- **People:** 12 crew members with daily schedules, friendship hearts, gift preferences, birthdays and letters.
- **Controls:** keyboard and mouse, touch (virtual joystick, look by dragging, action buttons) and gamepad (including full menu navigation). Fullscreen is on by default and can be toggled on the title screen or in Settings.
- **Saving:** autosaves every 30 seconds, when you sleep and when the tab is hidden. Saves can be exported and imported in Settings.

# Hamnljus · Harbor Light

A cozy, story-driven 2D platformer RPG set in the sleepy Swedish harbor village of Saltvik. Everything is in a single file: open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** You inherit Grandma Ingrid's farm. Restore the harbor bit by bit and relight the old lighthouse (21-step main quest plus 26 side quests).
- **Activities:** farming (20 crops, 4 seasons, greenhouse), fishing (38 species incl. legendaries, reel minigame), foraging, mining, tree chopping, cooking (28 dishes), crafting, artisan machines, animals.
- **World:** 8 areas (farm, harbor, forest, beach, cliffs, cave, Lighthouse Isle, greenhouse), day/night cycle, weather, 4 seasonal festivals.
- **People:** 12 villagers with schedules, friendship hearts, gift tastes and rewards.
- **Long-term goals:** 9 harbor projects, museum, 30 hidden golden shells, daily requests, 22 achievements, tool upgrades.
- **Languages:** Svenska and English (switch on the title screen or in Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard, touch (on-screen D-pad and buttons) and gamepad. Gamepad: A jump / back, B use & talk / confirm, Y menu, LB/RB cycle items. Keyboard: E confirm, Space back. Touch: ✋ confirm, ⤒ back. Fullscreen is on by default (entered on your first tap/click; toggle in Settings).
- **Saving:** autosaves to browser storage every 30 seconds, when you sleep, and when the tab is hidden; "Save & exit" in the menu returns to the title screen. Saves can be exported/imported as text in Settings.
