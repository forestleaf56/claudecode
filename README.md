# Hamnljus · Harbor Light

A cozy, story-driven 2D platformer RPG set in the sleepy Swedish harbor village of Saltvik. Everything is in a single file: open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** You inherit Grandma Ingrid's farm. Restore the harbor bit by bit and relight the old lighthouse (21-step main quest plus 26 side quests).
- **Activities:** farming (20 crops, 4 seasons, greenhouse), fishing (38 species incl. legendaries, reel minigame), foraging, mining, tree chopping, cooking (28 dishes), crafting, artisan machines, animals.
- **World:** 8 areas (farm, harbor, forest, beach, cliffs, cave, Lighthouse Isle, greenhouse), day/night cycle, weather, 4 seasonal festivals.
- **People:** 12 villagers with schedules, friendship hearts, gift tastes and rewards.
- **Long-term goals:** 9 harbor projects, museum, 30 hidden golden shells, daily requests, 22 achievements, tool upgrades.
- **Languages:** Svenska and English (switch on the title screen or in Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard, touch (on-screen D-pad and buttons) and gamepad. Gamepad: A jump / back, B use & talk / confirm, Y menu, LB/RB cycle items. Keyboard: E confirm, Space back. Touch: ✋ confirm, ⤒ back. Fullscreen is on by default (entered on your first tap/click; toggle in Settings).
- **Saving:** autosaves to browser storage every 30 seconds, when you sleep, and when the tab is hidden; "Save & exit" in the menu returns to the title screen. Saves can be exported/imported as text in Settings.

# Starhaven · Stjärnhamn

A second, separate game in `starhaven.html`: a cozy, slow-paced, story-driven 2D platformer RPG aboard a drifting generation spaceship. Open `starhaven.html` in any modern browser.

- **Story:** You wake as the ship's caretaker. Restore 10 ship systems deck by deck, research Warp Theory and steer the crew home to the green planet Verdance (21 main quests + 35 side quests, ending + post-game).
- **13 decks:** Cabin, Bridge, Hydroponics, Engineering, Promenade, Cargo Hold, Observatory, Aquarium, Ranch, Greenhouse Dome, Outer Hull (low gravity), Reactor Core, and the planet Verdance. Multi-level layouts with ladders, platforms, hidden star shards and Earth artifacts.
- **Systems:** farming (35 crops, regrowing crops, fertilizer, dome-only exotics), mining and gathering, smelting, crafting, cooking (34 dishes), 23 research projects, 15 ship building projects, furniture placement with cabin comfort, animals, bee hives, fishing minigame (17 catches), shops, daily request board, 200+ items, a 170-item archive collection, and 13 crew members with friendship hearts, gift tastes and heart rewards.
- **Languages:** English and Svenska (title screen or Settings).
- **Controls:** keyboard, touch (D-pad, ⤒ jump, ✋ use) and gamepad (B confirms, uses and talks; A jumps, cancels and goes back; Y bag, Start menu, LB/RB tabs, right stick or LT/RT scrolls menus). Fullscreen on by default.
- **Quest tracker:** shows up to two active quests with every goal ticked off and where each person is.
- **Drawn icons:** every icon (items, crops, fish, animals, furniture, UI symbols, flags) is hand-drawn vector art, so the game uses no emoji at all and looks the same on every device.
- **Splash:** opens with the "Cozy Forest" splash screen (shared with Hamnljus).
- **Saving:** 3 slots, autosave every 30 seconds, plus saving when you sleep and when the tab is hidden.
- **Look & sound:** storybook paper-cut art style (warm gouache colors, ink outlines on characters, icons and floors, paper grain, parchment menus with sticker buttons) with a distinct theme for every area (wood-panelled cabin with curtains and fairy lights, bridge viewscreen and consoles, vine-covered hydroponics with grow lights, gears and pipes in engineering, market stalls and string lights on the Promenade, crates and hazard stripes in cargo, constellations and orreries in the observatory, fish tanks in the aquarium, barn windows and hay bales on the ranch, sunlit trellises in the dome, solar panels on the hull, glowing conduits in the reactor, trees on Verdance), themed floors, lights and ambient particles, and a generative soundtrack whose mood changes per area, day and night.
