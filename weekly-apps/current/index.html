MIRRORBLOOM
paint with symmetry

How it works

  • Drag on the canvas to paint — every stroke is mirrored across the sectors into a symmetric bloom.
  • Tap symmetry to change the number of sectors, mirror for kaleidoscope reflection, palette and brush to change the look.
  • Press ❋ Bloom for an instant generative piece — a fresh one every time.
  • Save keeps a piece in your gallery; Export downloads it as a PNG; Undo and Clear help you iterate.
