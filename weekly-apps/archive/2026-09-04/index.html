How to delve

  • Tap a tile next to you, use the on-screen d-pad, or the arrow/WASD keys to move.
  • Move into an enemy to strike it. Everything acts only when you do — take your time.
  • Step onto ◈ gold, ❤ potions, weapons, armor, ≈ flame scrolls (scorch nearby foes) and ◆ whetstones (+1 attack) to grab them. Better gear replaces weaker gear.
  • Detour to a glowing ✦ shrine for a one-time boon, and beware hidden traps. Every 5th floor a Warden guards the way down.
  • Find the ▼ stairs and descend. Each floor is generated fresh and grows deadlier — and richer.
  • Death is permanent. The only goal is to reach a deeper floor than last time.
