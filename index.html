"We just need one big red button," a lawmaker explained, gesturing at a diagram of a cloud connected to another cloud connected to a third, on-fire cloud. Aides report the AI in question is "in the cloud," and the cloud is, unhelpfully, everywhere and nowhere, like a rumor or a draft.

🧊 Google Reveals "Frozen" Silicon To Cut A.I. Costs; Chip Told To "Just Chill"

MOUNTAIN VIEW — reported from beside a very cold server

HARDWARE — Google is racing to slash inference costs with fresh Gemini models and its own custom "Frozen" silicon, a chip so named because it is cool, efficient, and refuses to let anything go.

"We told it to relax and stop being expensive, and honestly? It listened," an engineer beamed. Rivals scrambled to announce their own chilled chips, including one reportedly named "Room Temperature" and another simply called "Please."

Analysts note the real innovation is a datacenter that finally costs slightly less than launching a satellite made of solid gold.
